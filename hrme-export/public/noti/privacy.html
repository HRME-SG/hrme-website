This Privacy Policy applies to the NOTI mobile application and associated services (collectively, "NOTI"), operated by HRME Pte. Ltd. By using NOTI, you agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy.

1. Our Approach to Privacy

NOTI does not sell your personal data. We collect only what is necessary to provide the NOTI service and do not use your information for third-party advertising.

2. What Data We Collect

When You Create an Account

Required:

  • Name and date of birth
  • Gender
  • Email address (via Google or Apple sign-in)

Optional:

  • Profile photos
  • Sexuality and personal preferences
  • Height, languages spoken, and what you are looking for
  • Bio and Noti status

When You Use NOTI

  • Location: Approximate location to show nearby profiles. You can disable this in your device settings at any time.
  • Activity: Swipes, matches, messages, and Bites.
  • Device data: Device type, operating system, push notification token.
  • Payments: Processed by Apple Inc. (App Store) or Google LLC (Google Play). We do not receive or store your payment card details.

From Third Parties

  • Google / Apple: Name and email when you sign in.
  • Apple / Google (payments): Payment confirmation and transaction records.
  • Push notification services: Delivery tokens for notifications.

3. Sensitive Data

NOTI may collect information that is considered sensitive in nature, including your sexuality and personal preferences. You provide this information voluntarily to enhance your experience on NOTI. By choosing to share this information, you consent to us processing it as described in this policy. We treat sensitive data with the highest level of care and do not share it with third parties except as necessary to operate the service.

4. How We Use Your Data

  • To provide and operate NOTI, including matching, messaging, and Bites
  • To show you relevant profiles based on your preferences and location
  • To process payments for Bites and NOTI+ subscriptions
  • To send push notifications for matches, messages, Bites, and Likes
  • To detect and prevent fraud, spam, and inappropriate content — including automated and manual review of content where necessary to keep our community safe
  • To improve and develop the NOTI service
  • To comply with applicable law and respond to lawful requests

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share limited data only with trusted third-party service providers who help us operate, maintain, and improve NOTI — such as database hosting, payment processing, push notification delivery, and app distribution. These providers are only permitted to use your data as necessary to perform services on our behalf.

We may also disclose your data to law enforcement or relevant authorities where required by law or to protect the safety of our users.

6. Tracking & Analytics

We may use analytics tools to understand how users interact with NOTI, identify issues, and improve the service. This may include information about how you navigate the app, features you use, and technical performance data. This data is used internally and is not linked to your identity for advertising purposes.

7. Security

We use industry-standard security measures including encrypted data transmission, database-level access controls, and secure payment processing. While we take reasonable steps to protect your data, no system is completely secure.

8. Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide you with NOTI's services. If you delete your account, we will delete or anonymise your personal data within a reasonable period, except where we are required to retain it by applicable law, to resolve disputes, or to prevent fraud or abuse. Anonymised or aggregated data that cannot identify you may be retained indefinitely for analytical purposes.

9. Account Deletion

You can delete your account at any time via the Profile tab in the app. When you delete your account, your profile is removed from NOTI and will no longer be visible to other users. Some data may be retained for a limited period as necessary to comply with legal obligations or to protect against fraudulent activity.

10. Children's Privacy

NOTI is strictly for users aged 18 and above. We do not knowingly collect data from minors. If you believe a minor has registered, contact us immediately at noti@hrme.sg.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy at any time. The most current version is always available within the app and on our website. Your continued use of NOTI constitutes acceptance of the current policy. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

12. Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact us at noti@hrme.sg.
